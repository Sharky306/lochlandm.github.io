Claude Mail Helper – Privacy Policy

Claude Mail Helper is a private tool used only by its owner, Lochland M. (lochlandm@gmail.com), to access the owner’s own Gmail and Google Calendar from their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed: email messages, labels, drafts, and calendar events in the owner’s own Google accounts, only when the owner asks.

Storage: sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner’s computer. The app runs no server, and no data is sold or shared with third parties by this app.
